A planner for the next 12 weeks

Your year is 12 weeks long.

Pick a few goals, plan once, and tick your way through twelve weeks. Shortyear turns your plan into daily tasks, and shows you every week whether you are really doing it.

85%a week is the target

How it works

  1. 01

    Choose what matters

    1 to 3 goals for the next twelve weeks. Few enough that you can actually move them.

  2. 02

    Plan once

    For each goal, add what you will repeat every week. Shortyear fills all twelve weeks with the tasks.

  3. 03

    Tick, and aim for 85%

    Check off what you did each day. Reach 85% of your week and your goals move. 100% is not the point.

Inside the app

  • A focus timer

    Time your deep work from any task. Lock your phone: the timer stays on your lock screen.

  • Challenges and badges

    Join a ready-made plan, like running your first race or building an emergency fund, and earn its badge in bronze, silver or gold.

  • One weekly review

    At the end of each week: what worked, what did not, and what changes for the week ahead.

  • Life balance

    Rate each area of your life and see what your twelve weeks leave aside.

  • Widgets and calendar

    Today, Score and Week widgets on your home screen. Your planned blocks can go straight into your calendar.

  • No ads, no tracking

    Your plans are yours. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.

Twelve weeks start whenever you do.
