Refund policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

If Shortyear is not right for you, you can get your money back. No questions asked.

14 days after every payment

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any payment: your first payment, including the one that follows the free trial, and every renewal. Write to support@shortyear.com from the email address of your account, or the one on your receipt. You don't need to give a reason.

The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Your Pro access ends when the refund is made.

The free trial

The yearly plan starts with 9 days free. You are not charged if you cancel before the trial ends, and the app reminds you the day before your plan starts.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for. To be refunded for the current period as well, ask within 14 days of the payment.

Where you bought it

  • On the web: payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. We ask Paddle to refund you, and the refund appears on your statement under Paddle.
  • In the Android app: payments are processed by Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policies. Request one from Google Play directly.

Your rights

This policy does not limit the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.
